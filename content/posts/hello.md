This is the first post. I wanted a place of my own to keep thoughts and experiences in one spot, in a form that is simple, readable and mine.

Feeds are good at the moment and bad at memory. A post is gone in a day. A page on my own site stays where I left it, and I can link back to it years from now.

Expect short notes more than polished essays: what I'm learning, what worked, what didn't. I'll write about three kinds of things: what I build, what I learn, and how I think. The first is the easiest to explain. The last is the hardest, which is probably why it's worth doing.

Every post here is a plain Markdown file in a public repository. There's no schedule. I'd rather publish something when it's ready than something every Friday.

Let's see what's worth writing down.
