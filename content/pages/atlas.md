---
title: Atlas
description: Trips in one place: itinerary, bookings, costs, places on a map.
---

Atlas keeps each trip as a folder of notes and puts the pieces together: the itinerary day by day, bookings and tickets, who paid for what, and the places worth seeing on a map.

Costs can stay in sync with a spreadsheet shared with the people you travel with.

> **Private repository.** The code is not public yet. If you would like to try it, [get in touch](/about).
