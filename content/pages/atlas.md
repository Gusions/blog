Planning a trip means scattered files: tickets in your email, costs in a spreadsheet, a list of places in a note. Atlas keeps each trip as a folder of notes in your vault and puts the pieces together.

## What it does

- **Overview.** Dates, destinations and the numbers that matter, at a glance.
- **Itinerary.** The trip day by day, stop by stop.
- **Bookings and documents.** Flights, trains, stays and the copies of your tickets, in one place.
- **Costs.** Every expense with who paid for it, split between travellers, with a clear balance of who owes whom. Only paid expenses count towards the balance.
- **Places.** Restaurants, bars and sights as notes, shown on a map.

## How it works

Each part of the trip is a plain note: overview, itinerary, logistics, budget, attachments. Costs can stay in sync with a Google Sheet, so people who don't use Obsidian can still add expenses. It works on desktop and mobile.
