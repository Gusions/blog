---

A record collection deserves better than a spreadsheet. Crate keeps each record as a note in your vault and turns the whole folder into a collection you can browse like a shop.

## What it does

- **Browse.** Scroll the collection with covers, or narrow it down by artist, genre, label, decade or tag.
- **Know where things are.** A sidebar shows where each record is stored, shelf by shelf, so you can find it in the real world too.
- **Covers.** Crate finds cover art on its own. No account is needed; you can add a Discogs token for releases it can't find.
- **Import.** Start from a CSV export of an existing collection.

## How it works

Every record stays a plain note with its details in the front matter, so you can edit, link and search them like any other note. Crate follows your Obsidian theme and accent colour, and its interface is available in English and Italian.
