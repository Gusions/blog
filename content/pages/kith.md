title: Kith
description: A personal CRM made of Markdown notes.
---

Most CRMs ask you to move your contacts into their app. Kith works the other way round: every person is a Markdown note in your vault, and Kith gives those notes the views and tools of a real CRM.

## What it does

- **Views.** See your people as a table, cards, a kanban board or a network graph. Save the views you use often.
- **People and companies.** Open a person to see their details, notes and a timeline of your interactions. Open a company to see who works there. Everything can be edited in place.
- **Import.** Bring in contacts from LinkedIn, vCard, Google CSV and Apple Contacts exports. Kith matches the same person across sources by email, profile and name, so you don't end up with duplicates.
- **Reminders.** It tells you whose birthday is coming up and who you haven't talked to in a while.
- **Clean data.** Values that mean the same thing are treated as one: Milano and Milan are the same city.

## How it works

Kith reads a folder of notes and their front matter. It never locks your data into its own format. Remove the plugin and you lose the views, not the people. The idea behind it is in [[your-contacts-are-just-notes|Your contacts are just notes]].

It works on desktop and mobile, and has a navigation sidebar for quick views and filters.

## Get it

- Code and releases: [GitHub](https://github.com/Gusions/obsidian-kith)
- Install with [BRAT](https://github.com/TfTHacker/obsidian42-brat) using `Gusions/obsidian-kith`, or copy the release files into `.obsidian/plugins/kith/`.
