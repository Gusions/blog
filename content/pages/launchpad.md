title: Launchpad
description: A start page for Obsidian that opens your plugins like apps.
---

Some Obsidian plugins are small apps in their own right: a CRM, a record collection, a finance dashboard, a travel planner. Once you have a few of them, opening Obsidian starts with a question: where do I go first? The ribbon icons and the command palette work, but they don't feel like a home. Launchpad gives Obsidian a start page.

## What it does

- **A home screen.** Your plugins appear as large app icons on a clean page, with a short greeting. Pick one, or choose to carry on with your notes as usual.
- **Keyboard first.** Press 1 to 9 to open an app, 0 to go straight to normal Obsidian, / to search, Esc to clear.
- **Your list.** Add, remove and reorder the apps. Launchpad can detect the commands of installed plugins, so adding one takes a click.
- **Startup.** Choose whether Launchpad opens every time Obsidian starts, or only when you call it.

## Design

Launchpad looks like the app launcher on a Mac: large icons, generous space and nothing else on the page. It respects the safe areas of phones and works on mobile as well as on desktop. The interface is available in English and Italian.

It doesn't change how your plugins work or where your notes live. It is only a door.

Requires Obsidian 1.10 or later.
