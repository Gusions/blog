Once you have several plugins that work like apps, opening Obsidian becomes a question: where do I start? Launchpad answers it with a start page.

## What it does

- **Home screen.** Your plugins appear as app icons, with a short greeting. Pick one, or carry on with plain notes.
- **Keyboard.** Press 1 to 9 to open an app, 0 for normal Obsidian, / to search, Esc to clear.
- **Your list.** Add, remove and reorder apps, or let Launchpad detect the commands of installed plugins.
- **Startup.** Choose whether it opens every time Obsidian starts.

It works on desktop and mobile, in English and Italian.
