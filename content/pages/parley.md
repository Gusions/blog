---
title: Parley
description: Reread exported WhatsApp and Telegram chats, and keep them as notes.
---

An exported chat is a text file and a pile of media. Parley opens it and shows it as a chat again: bubbles, days, photos and voice notes where they were sent. You can search a conversation, jump to a date and browse its media in a gallery.

When you want, it writes a chat into the vault as plain Markdown, one note per month. The media stays in the export, so the vault stays light. The full story is in [[chats-you-can-still-read|Chats you can still read]].

## Get it

- Code and releases: [GitHub](https://github.com/Gusions/obsidian-parley)

Desktop first. Mobile is experimental.
