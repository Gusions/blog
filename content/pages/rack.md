If you keep your clothes as notes, one per garment with a photo, Rack turns that folder into a closet you can actually look at, and helps you decide what to wear.

## What it does

- **Closet.** A photo grid of everything you own. Filter by season, colour, brand, size or shop, or search by name.
- **Outfits.** A board where you put pieces together. Rack can suggest an outfit for warm, mild or cold weather; lock the pieces you want to keep and let it fill the rest.
- **Rules.** Suggestions follow a few sensible rules: no two layers of the same kind, no two patterns together, shorts and short sleeves only in summer. You can exclude garments, like clothes you only wear at home, from suggestions.
- **Stats.** What you spent by category, brand, shop and year, your colours and seasons, what you wear most and the cost per wear.

## How it works

Every garment stays a plain note. When you edit a piece in Rack, the change is written back to the note's front matter. It works on desktop and mobile. The idea behind it is in [[notes-cant-show-a-jacket|Notes can't show a jacket]].
