---
title: Rack
description: Your wardrobe as a closet and an outfit builder.
---

Rack turns garment notes, each with a photo, into a closet you can filter by season, colour or brand. It builds outfits from what you own, based on the weather, and a few rules keep the suggestions sensible: shorts only in summer, no two layers of the same kind.

Edits made in Rack are written back to the notes. The idea behind it is in [[notes-cant-show-a-jacket|Notes can't show a jacket]].

> **Private repository.** The code is not public yet. If you would like to try it, [get in touch](/about).
