---
title: Rack
description: My wardrobe as a closet and an outfit builder.
---

Every garment is a note with a photo. Rack shows them as a closet you can filter by season, colour or brand, and builds outfits from what you own, based on the weather. A few rules keep the suggestions sane: shorts only in summer, no two layers of the same kind.

Edits made in Rack go back into the notes. Why I wanted it is in [[notes-cant-show-a-jacket|Notes can't show a jacket]].

Personal project, not published.
