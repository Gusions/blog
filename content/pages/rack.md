title: Rack
description: A visual wardrobe for Obsidian: a closet, an outfit builder and the numbers behind what you wear.
---

If you keep your clothes in Obsidian, one note per garment with a photo, you have a tidy list that tells you very little. A note can say you own a jacket; it can't show you how it looks next to the trousers you are about to put on. Rack reads those notes and turns them into a closet you can look at, an outfit board and a set of stats. The idea behind it is in [[notes-cant-show-a-jacket|Notes can't show a jacket]].

## Closet

Every garment appears as a photo in a grid. Filter by category, season, colour, brand, size or shop, search by name, and narrow down by type: jeans, cargo or chinos for trousers, long or short sleeves for shirts. Open a piece to see its details in a large view, and edit everything there, from the price to the season. Changes are written back to the note, and renaming a garment or changing its category moves the note to the right place.

## Outfits

The outfit board has a slot for each layer: jacket, shirt, t-shirt, trousers, shoes and accessories. Fill it by hand, or ask Rack for a suggestion for warm, mild or cold weather. Lock the pieces you want to keep and let it fill the rest.

Suggestions follow a few rules that keep them wearable:

- no two layers of the same kind, like two sweatshirts;
- no two patterns together;
- shorts and short-sleeved shirts only in summer;
- clothes you mark as home-only never appear in suggestions, but you can still pick them yourself.

## Stats

What you spent, by category, brand, shop and year; which colours and seasons dominate your wardrobe; what you wear most; and the cost per wear of each piece. Gifts are counted as garments, not as spending.

## How it works

Each garment stays a plain note with its details in the front matter and its photos as attachments. Rack generates light thumbnails so the grid stays fast, on desktop and on mobile. Remove the plugin and your notes are exactly as they were.
